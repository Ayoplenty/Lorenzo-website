Two ways to connect

Pick the one that fits how much you depend on the grid today.

Hybrid (Backup)

Solar behind your grid supply

  • Switches to the solar hub on its own when the main grid goes out
  • Same app, same pay-per-use credit
  • Good for shops and homes that mostly have grid power
Primary

Solar as your main supply

  • Your line runs from the hub with solar and battery
  • Plan loads with the appliance estimator
  • Good where the grid is rarely there

Connection fees and power limits for each type will be confirmed before launch.

Questions people ask

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay a one-time connection fee, then buy credit and pay only for the energy you use.

What happens when my credit runs out?

Supply stops until you top up, so Lorenzo warns you when your balance runs low. You can also turn on auto top-up to avoid it.

Can I use Lorenzo without mobile data?

Yes. The app shows your last known balance offline and has a low-data mode. A USSD option lets you top up and report outages from any phone.

Can my family top up my meter?

Yes. The account holder adds household members and chooses whether each one can top up or only view.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. Rates, connection fees and power limits shown here are examples. The pilot will confirm them before launch.
